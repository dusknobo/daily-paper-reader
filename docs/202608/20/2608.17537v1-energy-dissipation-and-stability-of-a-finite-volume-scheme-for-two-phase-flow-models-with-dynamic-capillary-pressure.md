## 摘要
针对退化伪抛物交叉扩散方程，提出并分析了一种隐式欧拉有限体积格式。该系统描述了多孔介质中具有动态毛细管压力的非饱和两相流混合物的动力学。数值格式基于两点通量近似，保持能量结构，确保总质量守恒，并保证水饱和度的严格正性和有界性。这些性质依赖于为非线性分量仔细选择的均值函数。证明了离散解的存在唯一性以及额外的网格一致有界性。二维空间中的数值实验展示了动态毛细管压力的影响。

## Abstract
An implicit Euler finite-volume scheme for degenerate pseudo-parabolic cross-diffusion equations is proposed and analyzed. The system describes the dynamics of an unsaturated two-phase flow mixture with dynamic capillary pressure in a porous medium. The numerical scheme is based on a two-point flux approximation that preserves the energy structure, ensures the conservation of total mass, and guarantees strict positivity and boundedness of the water saturation. These properties rely on carefully selected mean functions for the nonlinear components. The existence and uniqueness of a discrete solution and additional mesh-uniform bounds are proved. Numerical experiments in two space dimensions illustrate the effects of the dynamic capillary pressure.
