## 摘要
熔体对流在合金凝固过程中的微观组织演化中起着关键作用，但准确捕捉其与移动固液界面的相互作用，尤其是在多相、多组分体系中，仍然是一个重大的计算挑战。本研究开发了一个计算框架，将巨势相场模型与格子玻尔兹曼方法（LBM）耦合，以在统一且热力学一致的公式中模拟对流驱动的凝固。该方法在演化的固液界面上严格施加无滑移条件，并使用标准的单松弛时间 Bhatnagar-Gross-Krook 碰撞算子求解流体输运。该框架为解决界面、溶质输运和流体流动的耦合演化提供了一种高效且稳健的方法。通过自然对流下枝晶和共晶凝固的模拟展示了该框架的通用性。结果表明，对流显著改变溶质偏析，使生长前沿失稳，并在两种体系中诱导振荡生长模式。这些示例说明了该方法捕捉复杂流动诱导的形态不稳定性的能力，并为合金凝固中对流驱动微观组织演化的机制提供了新见解。

## Abstract
Melt convection plays a critical role in microstructure evolution during alloy solidification, yet accurately capturing its interaction with moving solid-liquid interfaces remains a significant computational challenge, particularly in multi-phase, multi-component systems. In this work, we develop a computational framework that couples a Grand-Potential phase-field model with the Lattice Boltzmann method (LBM) to simulate convection-driven solidification within a unified and thermodynamically consistent formulation. The proposed approach rigorously enforces no-slip conditions at evolving solid-liquid interfaces, while fluid transport is solved using the standard single-relaxation-time Bhatnagar-Gross-Krook collision operator. The framework provides an efficient and robust methodology for resolving the coupled evolution of interfaces, solute transport, and fluid flow.   The versatility of the proposed framework is demonstrated through simulations of dendritic and eutectic solidification under natural convection. The results show that convection significantly modifies solute segregation, destabilizes growth fronts, and induces oscillatory growth modes in both systems. These examples illustrate the capability of the proposed method to capture complex flow-induced morphological instabilities and provide new insights into the mechanisms governing convection-driven microstructure evolution in alloy solidification.
