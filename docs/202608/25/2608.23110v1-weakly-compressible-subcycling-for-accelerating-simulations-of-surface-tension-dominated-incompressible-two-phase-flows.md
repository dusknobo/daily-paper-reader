## 摘要
表面张力主导的不可压缩两相流模拟因毛细时间步长约束严重而计算开销很大。尽管许多研究提出了表面张力的时间隐式离散，以允许采用更大的时间步长并加速模拟，但这些方法要么存在人工耗散，要么实现复杂。在此，我们提出一种简单而新颖的方法：带弱可压缩子循环的不可压缩求解器。该方法放宽了毛细时间步长约束，从而在无需借助人工耗散稳定化或复杂实现的情况下将模拟加速超过 8.6×。其核心思想是引入使用弱可压缩求解器的轻量子步来辅助主不可压缩求解器。这些子步使主不可压缩求解器即使在较大时间步长下也能使用精确计算的通量和表面张力。数值测试证明了该方法在实际问题中的有效性，包括 Rayleigh--Plateau 不稳定性和多孔介质中的两相流。本研究为一种新范式铺平了道路：弱可压缩求解器可作为不可压缩求解器的助手。

## Abstract
Simulations of surface-tension-dominated incompressible two-phase flows are computationally expensive due to the severe capillary time-step constraint. Although many studies have proposed time-implicit discretizations of surface tension to allow larger time-step sizes and accelerate simulations, these methods suffer from either artificial dissipation or complex implementation. Here, we propose a simple and novel approach: an incompressible solver with weakly compressible subcycling. The proposed approach relaxes the capillary time-step constraint, thereby accelerating simulations by more than $8.6\times$ without relying on artificially dissipative stabilization or requiring complex implementation. The key idea is to introduce lightweight substeps using a weakly compressible solver to assist the main incompressible solver. These substeps enable the main incompressible solver to use accurately computed fluxes and surface tension force, even with large time-step sizes. Numerical tests demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach for practical problems, including the Rayleigh--Plateau instability and two-phase flows in porous media. This study paves the way for a new paradigm in which a weakly compressible solver serves as an assistant to an incompressible solver.
