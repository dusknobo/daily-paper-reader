## 摘要
我们开展了亚临界雷诺数（Re=10,000）下球体通气空化的直接数值模拟（DNS）。流动被建模为水与空气的均匀混合物，界面采用流体体积法追踪。与以往的数值研究不同，本工作显式考虑了表面张力。空气通过球面上的环形条带注入，根据总体流动动力学选取了三个注入位置：前部（FR025，18°≤θ≤63°）、中部（MD025、MD050，75°≤θ≤104°）和后部（BK025，120°≤θ≤180°），注入系数为C_q=0.2和0.4。注入位置强烈控制空泡的起始与稳定性，并改变了前部和中部注入情形下的流动分离。前部注入因剧烈的喷发产生非稳态泡状空腔，使注入空气破碎，阻力相对于单相情形增加约56%。相反，中部和后部注入产生稳定空腔，并具有不同的前缘动力学特征。在中部注入情形中，喷发占主导，尽管流动分离被推迟，空腔仍从注入区的前缘脱落。对于后部注入，由于空腔形成引起的逆压力梯度，空腔在注入区上游脱落。开尔文-亥姆霍兹不稳定性和凹坑形成是后部注入空腔的典型特征。稳定空腔表现出强烈的空气卷吸，并通过回射流闭合。中部注入情形在C_q=0.2和0.4时分别实现35%和25%的减阻，而后部注入相对于单相情形最大减阻达46%。

## Abstract
We perform DNS of ventilated cavitation over a sphere at subcritical (Re=10,000). The flow is modeled as a homogeneous mixture of water and air, with the interface tracked using the volume-of-fluid method. Unlike previous numerical investigations, surface tension is explicitly included. Air is injected through a circular strip on the sphere at three locations selected based on the bulk flow dynamics: front (FR025, (18^\circ\leqθ\leq63^\circ)), mid (MD025, MD050, (75^\circ\leqθ\leq104^\circ)), and back (BK025, (120^\circ\leqθ\leq180^\circ)), with (C_q=0.2) and (0.4). The injection location strongly governs cavity inception and stability and alters flow separation in the front- and mid-injection cases. Front injection produces an unsteady bubbly cavity due to vigorous puffing, which fragments the injected air and increases drag by (\sim56%) relative to the single-phase case. In contrast, mid- and back-injection produce stable cavities with distinct leading-edge dynamics. In the mid-injection cases, puffing dominates and, despite delayed flow separation, the cavity detaches from the leading edge of the injection patch. For back injection, the cavity detaches upstream of the injection patch owing to the adverse pressure gradient induced by cavity formation. Kelvin--Helmholtz instabilities and divot formation characterize the back-injection cavity. Stable cavities exhibit strong air entrainment and close through a re-entrant jet. The mid-injection cases achieve (35%) and (25%) drag reduction at (C_q=0.2) and (0.4), respectively, while back injection yields a maximum drag reduction of (46%) relative to the single-phase case.
